This is a sample post so the **Writing** section is not empty.

## What goes here

I will use this space for notes, short essays, and other writing that does not fit the résumé sections of the homepage.

## How it works

1. Drop a Markdown file into the `writing/` folder.
2. Put a short header at the top with `title`, `date`, and optional `summary`.
3. Run `./scripts/publish-writing.sh` to rebuild the index and push to GitHub.

You can delete this sample anytime and replace it with your own notes.
